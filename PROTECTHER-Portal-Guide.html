PROTECTHER Audit Panel

How the safety audit portal works — a guide for auditors & contractors/clients

🔗 https://audit.protecther.in

? What is this portal

One place to run safety audits and close out the findings — online, from any phone or computer.

A PROTECTHER auditor inspects a site and records what is compliant and what is not. Every problem found (a “Non-Compliance”) becomes a Correction — a task the contractor/client then fixes by uploading a photo of the fix. The auditor reviews and closes it. Everything is tracked in one place — no paper, no email chains.

1 Who uses it (roles)

Everyone logs in at the same website; what they see depends on their role.

👑 Admin

PROTECTHER office. Creates logins, oversees all projects, approves audits.

🦺 Auditor

Conducts the site audit, records findings with photos/notes, and submits the report.

🏗️ Client / Contractor

Sees only their own package’s problems, uploads photos of the fixes, submits for approval.

2 How to get a login (join the portal)

Logins are created by the PROTECTHER Admin — no self sign-up.

  1. Admin creates the user — Settings → Users → Add User. Pick the role (Auditor or Client) and, for a client, their package (e.g. C3-S1).
  2. You receive an email & a temporary password — from the PROTECTHER office.
  3. Log in — open https://audit.protecther.in and sign in.
  4. Change your password — top-right → Profile (or use “Forgot password” on the login page).
📱 Tip: On a phone you can “Add to Home Screen” so it opens like an app.

3 The full work-flow (how a problem gets fixed)

From site inspection to a closed, verified fix.

1
Auditor does the audit

Goes through the checklist and marks each point Compliant C or Non-Compliant NC.

2
Auditor adds proof for each NC

A photo of the problem, or a short written note if there’s nothing to photograph (e.g. a missing document).

3
Auditor submits the report

Every NC automatically becomes a Correction (a fix-task). The audit goes to “Pending Review”.

4
Client logs in & fixes

Sees only their package’s Corrections, does the work, and uploads a photo of the fix → taps Submit for review.

5
Auditor reviews the fix

Sees the “before” and “after” photos side by side → Approve (closes it) or Reject with a reason (sends it back).

✓
Correction closed

The item is fixed, verified, and recorded — with full photo evidence.

4 Step-by-step for each person

🦺 If you are an Auditor

  1. New Audit — pick the package & categories.
  2. Go point by point — tap each item, set C / NC / NA.
  3. For every NC — add a photo or type a one-line note in “Observation”.
  4. Save often — it auto-saves as you go.
  5. Submit — when done. If a photo/note is missing it tells you exactly which item.
  6. Later — review fixes — Corrections → Client Submissions → Approve or Reject.

🏗️ If you are a Client / Contractor

  1. Open “Items to Fix” — your only screen; lists your package’s problems.
  2. Read the finding — see the auditor’s note and photo.
  3. Do the fix on site.
  4. Add fix photo — tap the camera and take a photo of the corrected work.
  5. Submit for review — it goes to the auditor.
  6. If rejected — read the reason, redo, and re-submit.

5 The important rules to remember

📌 Every NC needs a photo OR a written note before the audit can be submitted. (Missing document? Just type a note — no photo needed.)
✅ Every NC automatically becomes a client Correction when the audit is submitted — nothing extra to tick.
🔒 A client sees only their own package — never other sites, never the full checklist, only the problems they must fix.
👀 The auditor always approves the fix — a client’s photo does not close an item until an auditor accepts it.

6 What the labels mean

C Compliant — all good NC Non-Compliant — needs fixing NA Not Applicable NV Not Verified — not checked yet 🟠 To Fix — client hasn’t submitted 🕓 In Review — waiting for auditor 🟢 Approved — closed & verified ↩️ Rejected — sent back to redo
